Token Radar

Pre-token protocols, ranked by what a launch would be worth — and how to farm each one.

Protocols whose token already trades are not on the board. They are the market the model calibrates against: each one's market cap over its TVL sets the multiple its category's pre-token protocols are scored with.

The model, in one line

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Category multiple — the median market-cap/TVL of the snapshot's live-token protocols in the category, each ratio winsorized to 0.005–5× so one tiny-TVL outlier cannot set a category, shrunk toward the global median (prior weight 5), clamped to 0.02–2.00. No multiple is hardcoded.
  • Growth — 1 + 0.5 × the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%..+100%.
  • Maturity — 0.65 + 0.35 × age capped at 3 years; a protocol that has run longer with no token scores higher.
  • Breadth — +2% per extra chain, up to +10%.

A protocol is on the board when its DefiLlama record carries no token and it is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of one that trades — the watchlist's rule, without its $50M floor. The board says a protocol records no token, never that it has none.
